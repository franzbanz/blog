### Task by Airbus future projects office

**Design of a Battery-Powered Commuter Aircraft**

Design a battery-electric powered, 19 seater commuter aircraft, which can be operated efficiently from short runways.

- integrate and evaluate technologies and design ideas on aircraft level to come up with a new and consistent aircraft concept
- **Objective**: Maximize the mission range with 19 pax payload, under the given contraints:
  - show a ladder chart, comparing your designs performance to the reference aircraft on a 100NM mission
  - choose and justify a cruise speed and altitude, and highlight the consequences in your aircraft design
  - present a concept for recharging, changing of batteries or another solution
  - ensure a minimum mission range of 100 NM. If you cannot reach 100NM reduce the number of Pax as necessary until you do

### Wing design

The wing design, in particular the wing span and to some extend the wing area are the biggest river for the aerodynamic efficiency of an aircraft.

design usually ends up shortly before drag divergence

### Improve Thermal efficiency, Improve propulsive efficiency, Engine Integration

Engine position is defined by several inputs

- aerodynamics
- structural integration
- space allocation
- safety

### Landing Gear Integration

The Main Landing Gear needs to be located aft of the most aft CG on ground and the wing needs to be located that the aircraft neutral point is aft of the most aft CG in flight (longitudinal stability criteria)

- fit cargo around landing gears
- dont interfere with propellers

### Some electric flight considerations

- its more than *best energy density*
  - consider safety, lifetime, power density
  - think about CG - battery placement
  - think about accessibility - battery replacement
- landing MLW - no fuel burned
- battery powered flight:
  - constant mass and engine efficiency
  - one cruise point to design for
  - speed and altitude - L/D
  - choose a good speed and altitude, also depends on mission length (climb)
- cruise altitude has knock on effects on aircraft design
- consider cruise altitude of a small commuter A/C
  - cabin pressurization
  - cross section - aero and structure weight
  - systems - weight and cost
