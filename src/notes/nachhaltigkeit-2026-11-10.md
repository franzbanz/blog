## Alternative Technical Concepts

- ACARE’s “Fly the Green Deal” set highly ambitious goals for emissions of CO2, NOx and noise
- “Radical technologies” in aircraft design and sustainable fuels aim for:
  - increased overall efficiency
  - no / less in-flight emissions
  - less community noise
  - viable operating cost and increased reliability

Aviation emissions about 1/3 "$CO_2$ effects", 2/3 "Non $CO_2$ effects"

### How to Change

The issue with full electric flight: Needed power for regional single-aisle aircraft is about **20MW**, way more than any currently flying electric aircraft

A lot of energy is needed even for single aisle commuter aircraft (more than 10000 kWh), while todays batteries have very low volumetric and gravimetric energy density compared to Jet A-1 fuel - Focus shift on hybrid-electric propulsion

### Hybrid Electric

Propeller is most efficient while rotating as slowly as possible and being as big as possible
A broad range of possible architectures:

- all-electric architecture
  - battery poweres motor, very simple
- serial architecture
  - fuel is used to power generator to generate electrical power
  - battery is used as well (serial) and can be used on its own or alongside generator (e.g. during take-off)
- parallel architecture
  - electric motor powered by battery poweres / supports combustion engine on same shaft
- turbo-electric architecture
  - no battery is used, only fuel is burned to run generator to run electric motor
  - can be used for hybrid approaches with very low hybridization degree (~3%) to vastly extend range by having the propeller in the back (electric motor is way smaller and lighter) or distributing many propellers over the wingspan

**Synergies are key to viable hybrid-electric flight**

also another problem: cooling fuel cells, which are not in the open air stream, like traditional engines

Aircraft design parameters affecting the figures of merit

- aircraft geometry
  - configuration
  - dimensions
  - masses
- hybrid-electric erchitecture
  - hybridization factor
  - selection of technologies
- energy management strategy
  - operation in different flight segments (all-electric take-off and landing, gas turbine optimized for cruise, electric boost for take-off, ...)
