in 2022, ICAO has set the long-term aspirtional goal for aviations net zero and highlighted the role of SAF in achieving it

There is general agreement among industry and government actors (...)

Globally, policy makers are adopting measures to expand SAF production and boost demand

Aviation fuel made up of...

- Paraffins
- Isoparaffins
- Naphtenes (Cyclo-paraffins)
- Aromatics
- Other atoms

SAF can be manufactured in different ways using a range of starting materials and processes

1. 1st gen saf - hydro processed esters and fatty acids (vegetable oils and inedible fats)
2. 2nd gen saf - waste based SAF (organic and municipal waste)
3. 3rd gen saf - power to liquid (renewable energy, green hydrogen and direct air capture)

SAF is a drop-in fuel, no need to invest in large infrastructure changes

### Economics

Currently, SAF prices are in the order of 2-3 times higher than conventional jet fuel (aviation biofuels)

Synthetic SAF estimates are in the order of 10 times higher than conventional jet fuel

SAF production facilities locations depend mostly on where the deefstock is coming from, and how it is supplied\

24% of SAF production capacity planned to be operational by 2026 has been completed, and 26% has been paused or canceled

an additional 94 bn dollars my be required to close the ambition gap in saf production
