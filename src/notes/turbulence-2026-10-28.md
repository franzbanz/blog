## Exact Transport Equations

- at high reynolds numbers, dissipation via turbulence dominates - except very close to walls
- $\varepsilon$ used in all modelss is the pseurdo-dissipation; it equals the true dissipation in homogeneous turbulence
- Notation: $\phi$ = viscous heat source in the energy equation $\varepsilon$ = turbulent dissipation rate
