### Maintenance

Manage and mitigate risks:
    
- Safety risks, faulty equipment is health hazard
- Risk to the environment, release of oil and grease
- Operation risk, increased downtime, lower performance
- Financial risk, loss of revenue

General definition: maintenance means methods for the determination and evaluation of the current status as well as the preservation and reestablishment of the nominal status of facilities, machines and components

- Reactive / Corrective maintenance
- Preventive maintenance
- Predictive maintenance

### Current Maintenance Practices for Wind Turbines

Compination of corrective and preventive maintenance\

- Onshore typically serviced twice a year
- Remote Offshore are serviced based on a database of components and their failure rate, variability of the site conditions, etc...
- Offshore Windfarm Operation and Maintenance contributes between 15-30% of the cost of energy, as they usually are less reliable
